Your heist, your choices

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Tiny Thief: Castle Heist is an iPhone stealth game made by Christoph Weidinger. The game has no account or sign-in and no in-app purchases. It stores your progress only on your device. Advertising is provided by Google.

Game progress and preferences

Completed heists, stars, best times, collected gems, unlocked field-guide pages and your sound setting are stored on your iPhone. The game does not sync them through iCloud and does not send them to the developer. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising

The game is free and uses Google AdMob to show full-screen interstitial ads between heists. Ads never appear after the first heist, never when you retry a heist, and at most once every 45 seconds.

Google’s advertising SDK may process your IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad interactions, crash information and performance data to deliver, measure and improve advertising and to help prevent fraud. Advertising providers may process information under their own policies.

See Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosures and Google’s Privacy Policy for details.

Consent and tracking

Where required, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game first shows Google’s consent message (User Messaging Platform) before any ads are requested.

Only afterwards, and only if you agreed in that message or no consent message is required, iOS may ask whether the app may track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose Ask App Not to Track, or tracking is not allowed, the game requests only non-personalised ads and Google does not receive the advertising identifier.

Your advertising choices

Where Google says it is required, Privacy choices appears in Settings so you can review or change your consent at any time. You can change the tracking permission in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Permissions and analytics

The game does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location. It includes no developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service. Google’s advertising SDK can process diagnostics as described above.

Children

The game is not specifically directed at children. Where consent is not given or tracking is not allowed, only non-personalised ads are requested.

This website and contacting support

This static website does not set its own cookies, use analytics scripts or load advertising. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose hosting infrastructure may process technical request information such as IP addresses. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments are used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details or other sensitive information. You may ask the developer to delete support correspondence, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Your data choices and rights

You can remove all game data by deleting the app and review your advertising choices from Settings. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of personal information, and to withdraw consent. Contact the developer about support correspondence or questions; Google and Apple provide privacy contacts for data they process. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.

Google, Apple and the website host operate internationally; information they process may be handled outside your country. Their privacy statements describe their practices and safeguards.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised date. For privacy questions, contact Christoph Weidinger, Austria, at christoph.weidinger.work@gmail.com.
