Tiny Thief:

72 heists · 9 wings · Made for iPhone

Stay out of the light

What you see is what they see.

Every guard carries a torch, and the cone of light is exactly what they can see. Walls, pillars, display cases and crates block it, so you always know where you are hidden.

  1. Sneak, or run

    Anywhere on the stick you sneak: slow and silent. Drag your thumb out to the red ring and hold it to run. You are faster, but every running step makes noise.

  2. Break their line of sight

    A guard who glimpses you comes over to look. One who sees you clearly gives chase. Duck behind cover and keep moving.

  3. Rest at the checkpoint

    Stand on the brass checkpoint deep in every hall. If you are caught, you start again from there with everything you had.

A gold arrow marks the treasure, and an arrow at the screen edge points the way when it is out of view.

Grab it, then run

The way in is the way out.

Taking the treasure trips the alarm. The vent opens, every guard starts hunting and a lockdown timer begins. Plan your escape before you grab.

  1. Race back to the vent

    Once the treasure is yours, the pointer turns mint and shows the way home. Reach the vent before the timer runs out.

  2. Use your gadgets

    Smoke makes the guards lose you, a quick dash rolls safely through lasers, decoy coins send everyone the wrong way and an EMP knocks out cameras, lasers and robot dogs for six seconds.

  3. Earn three stars

    Collect the gems, beat the par time and get out clean.

Robot dogs, wall cameras, pressure plates and keycard gates wait in the later wings.

A castle full of secrets

Easy to pick up. Tricky to master.

72 heists, 9 wings

From the Grand Gallery and the Armory down to the Crypt, through the Security Wing and the Vault, up to the rooftop terrace.

A gentle start

The first heist shows you around, and calmer practice wings sit between the harder ones, so the difficulty climbs one step at a time.

Know your enemy

Every guard, robot dog, camera and trap has its own page in the field guide, with its 3D model, and new ones are shown before the heist that brings them.

No lives, no energy, no waiting

Try a heist as often as you like. Your progress is saved on your iPhone, and the game speaks 34 languages, following your iPhone’s language.
