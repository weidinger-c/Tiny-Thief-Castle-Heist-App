Common questions

How do I play?

Move with the stick in the lower part of the screen. Find the treasure, marked by a floating gold arrow, take it, and get back out through the vent where you came in. If the treasure is off screen, an arrow at the edge of the screen points towards it.

How do I run?

Anywhere inside the stick you sneak, slowly and silently. Drag your thumb out to the red ring around the stick and hold it there to run. Running is faster but noisy: guards and sleepers nearby hear you.

Why did the guard see me?

A guard sees exactly what the torch cone lights up. Walls, pillars, display cases and crates block the light, so stay behind them. If a guard only glimpses you, they come over to look; if they see you clearly, they chase you. Sentries stand still but sweep a beam across the hall and call the guards when it touches you.

What happens when I take the treasure?

In most heists, grabbing the treasure trips the alarm. Every guard starts hunting and a timer shows how long you have before the castle locks down. The edge arrow turns mint and points back to the vent. Reach it before the timer runs out.

What are checkpoints?

Each hall has a brass checkpoint. Walk over it to light it. If you are caught afterwards, you restart from there instead of from the vent.

How do gadgets work?

Gadget buttons appear when a heist gives you some. Smoke makes guards lose you for five seconds, Dash rolls you quickly forward and safely through lasers, a Decoy coin draws everyone in earshot to where it lands, and EMP switches cameras, lasers and robot dogs off for six seconds. Smoke, Decoy and EMP show how many you have left; Dash recharges after each use.

How do I earn three stars?

Escape with the treasure, collect every gem in the heist and finish within the par time. You can replay any unlocked heist to improve.

How do I unlock the next wing?

There are 72 heists in 9 wings. Finishing a heist unlocks the next one, and finishing the last heist of a wing opens the next wing. Completed heists can be replayed at any time.

What is the field guide?

The Field Guide, on the home screen and in the pause menu, has a page for every guard, gadget and trap, with its 3D model and a short description. Before a heist that brings something new, its pages are shown to you first.

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress is saved only on your iPhone. There is no account and no iCloud sync. Deleting the app deletes your progress; moving to a new iPhone with a full device backup or transfer usually keeps it.

When do ads appear?

Between heists, never after the first one, never when you retry and at most once every 45 seconds. There are no purchases in the game.

How do I change my advertising consent?

Where Google says it is required, Privacy choices appears in Settings. Open it to review or change your choices. App tracking can be changed in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Can I change the language or turn off sound?

The game follows your iPhone’s language and supports 34 languages. To use a different language for this game only, open the iPhone Settings app, choose Apps → Tiny Thief → Language. Sound can be turned on or off in the game’s Settings.
